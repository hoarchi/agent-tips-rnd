# Protocol 06 - Paper Writing

## Goal

Write a paper whose claims are bounded by evidence.

## Steps

1. Start from the claim ledger.
2. Draft contribution statements.
3. Map each contribution to experiments and sources.
4. Draft method only after the benchmark and baselines are clear.
5. Include negative results and limitations.
6. Keep abstract and introduction weaker than the evidence, never stronger.
7. Prepare reproducibility appendix.

## Required Sections

- Abstract
- Introduction
- Related Work
- Task and Benchmark
- Method
- Experiments
- Results
- Error Analysis
- Limitations
- Ethical and Privacy Considerations
- Reproducibility Statement
