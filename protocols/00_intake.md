# Protocol 00 - Intake

## Goal

Convert ambiguous product or research intent into a neutral research brief.

## Steps

1. Read available handoff, product, phase, and strategy documents.
2. Extract observations without upgrading them into claims.
3. Identify desired decisions: product architecture, benchmark, paper, dataset,
   model choice, or funding/customer evidence.
4. Record constraints: privacy, cost, time, tooling, target venue, and data
   availability.
5. Define non-goals and forbidden shortcuts.
6. Write `research-brief.md`.

## Output Checklist

- Problem statement
- Seed observations
- Candidate research questions
- Assumptions log
- Sensitive-data boundary
- Immediate next artifact
