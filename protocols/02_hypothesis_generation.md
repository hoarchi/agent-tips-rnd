# Protocol 02 - Hypothesis Generation

## Goal

Generate falsifiable hypotheses, not slogans.

## Steps

1. Define the research question.
2. Write the null hypothesis.
3. Write the primary hypothesis.
4. Write at least two competing hypotheses.
5. Define variables and measurable outcomes.
6. Define what result would falsify or weaken the hypothesis.
7. Identify ethical, privacy, or deployment constraints.

## Quality Bar

A hypothesis is not ready unless a reasonable experiment could prove it wrong.
